## 1. Who must write it [RSE]

IDPC Rules (2026-01-08 consolidation; corpus `../../../research/sources/rules/idpc-rules.txt`), **Rule 2603(1)(i)**:
- (c) "Registered Representative (retail) dealing with securities: Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam and Retail Securities Exam"
- (d) "Registered Representative (retail) dealing with derivatives: Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam and Retail Securities Exam and Derivatives Exam"
- (i)(I) **Supervisor**: the Supervisor Exam plus "the exam requirements applicable to the individuals being supervised listed in sub-clauses 2603(1)(i)(a) to (f), and (h)". In practice this means the RSE for supervisors of retail RRs. The CIRE (not the RSE) is waived if the supervisor meets the 2-year experience requirement in 2603(1)(ii)(d).

**Not required for:** Investment Representatives, who need only the CIRE (2603(1)(i)(a)); institutional RRs, who take the Institutional Securities Exam ((e)/(f)); or Associate PMs and PMs. **Rule 2627(3)** exempts an applicant from "the Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam and the Retail Securities Exam and Institutional Securities Exam" if they satisfy the APM or PM designation requirements in 2603(1)(i)(g) or (h).

**Additional pre-approval requirement for RRs**, Rule 2603(1)(ii)(a): "A relevant diploma or degree from an accredited post secondary institution, or minimum four years of relevant experience acceptable to the Corporation."

**Mutual-funds-only RRs** at an investment-dealer-only firm, Rule 2605(1): before approval they may complete any of the following:
- the 2603(1)(i)(c) requirements (CIRE + RSE)
- the CSI Canadian Securities Course
- the IFSE Canadian Investment Funds Course
- the CSI Investment Funds in Canada Course

They must then "Complete the requirements in sub-clause 2603(1)(i)(c) [CIRE + RSE] and clause 2604(2)(i) within 270 days of approval" and "Upgrade within 18 months of initial approval."

**Transition rules:**
- **Rule 2629(2)**: an individual who was required to complete Wealth Management Essentials (WME) as of 2025-12-31 may instead complete the "Retail Securities Exam by the required completion date prescribed under the rules that were in effect prior to January 1, 2026."
- **Rule 2629(1)**: there is a CSI-course carve-out if the course and exam are completed and the application is submitted before 2027-01-01. CIRO's New Candidates FAQ summarises the same conditions: https://www.ciro.ca/registered-individuals/proficiency/new-candidates
- **Rule 2625(2)**: individuals approved before 2025-12-31 who stay in the same role are exempt from the new exam requirements. Conduct Training under 2604(2) still applies to them.

The RSE exam page defers "who will be required" to Bulletin 25-0212: https://www.ciro.ca/newsroom/publications/ciro-proficiency-model-approved-persons-under-investment-dealer-and-partially-consolidated-rules-2. That bulletin labels this exam "Retail Registered Representative (Securities)".

## 2. Format [RSE unless tagged]

The exam-parameters table in the official **Retail Securities Exam Syllabus** ("Last updated May 8, 2025") is at https://www.ciro.ca/media/13541/download?inline (local text: `sources/Retail-Securities-Exam-Syllabus-EN.txt`, page 2). It states:

| Parameter | Official value |
|---|---|
| Exams | **1 paper** |
| Exam format | **Proctored (remote or in person)** |
| Exam duration | **3 hours** (180 min, which works out to **1.5 min per question**; the Supervisor Exam allows 2.0) |
| Question format | **Multiple choice**. Unlike the Supervisor syllabus (60 standard + 30 item set), **no standard/item-set split is stated** |
| Questions per exam | **120** |
| Attempts allowed per exam | **3** |

**Question weighting ("Indicative Qs"):** these sum to 120. Elements 1 + 5 + 4 make up 57/120 (47.5%).

| Element | Title | Indicative Qs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Know-Your-Client (KYC) & suitability | 27 |
| 2 | Fixed income | 10 |
| 3 | Equities | 12 |
| 4 | Securities analysis | 14 |
| 5 | Managed products and other investments | 16 |
| 6 | Portfolio construction | 13 |
| 7 | Investment recommendations | 14 |
| 8 | Execution and market integrity | 7 |
| 9 | Monitoring, reporting and maintaining client relationships | 7 |

**Remaining format facts:**
- **Options per question:** **4 (A–D)**. Inferred from the official practice exam (see §3); the syllabus does not state it.
- **Item sets / case-based questions:** **None indicated.** The syllabus lists only "Multiple choice". The practice exam has no shared-scenario blocks and no stems that cross-reference other questions (§3). Treat all 120 as standalone questions. *(This differs from the Supervisor Exam.)* No official text explicitly says "no item sets", so this is an inference from official materials.
- **Taxonomy** (syllabus p. 3): Remember / Understand / Apply / Analyze. "The syllabus terms are cumulative. For example, learning outcomes using the Apply verb can also test on the levels of Understand and Remember." The Apply level explicitly includes "calculate", and the syllabus's own example questions include return and fee calculations.
- **Computational content is substantial** (syllabus element summaries):
  - E2 "requires an ability to calculate the price and yields of fixed-income securities"
  - E2.8: modified duration
  - E2.9 / E3.10 / E7.8: time value of money
  - E4: ratio calculations
  - E5: "may require candidates to calculate the value of" managed funds
  - E6.8: CAPM
  - E7: income tax payable, capital gains and losses
  - E9.3: performance calculations
- **Delivery** [REGIME, re-verified 2026-10-22]: computer-based. The two options are:
  - Prometric test centres in Canada: "In-person exams are administered at Prometric test centers across Canada" (https://fitchlearning-na.zendesk.com/hc/en-ca/articles/5188799562271-Where-can-I-take-the-exam)
  - Prometric ProProctor live remote proctoring
  
  CIRO FAQ: "Will exams be available online and in person? Yes." (https://www.ciro.ca/registered-individuals/proficiency/new-candidates)
- **Proctoring** [REGIME]: remote candidates "will be observed and monitored through continuous live video, screen sharing, and audio feeds". The exam timer is not paused during security checks (https://fitchlearning-na.zendesk.com/hc/en-ca/articles/6406885136031-Important-Exam-Security-Monitoring, updated 2026-10-09). Unscheduled breaks are allowed, but "the timer of your exam will still count down" (…/articles/5188809603615).
- **Languages** [REGIME, re-verified]: English and French. "Yes, all of the CIRO Exams are available in French and can be taken in French at any test centre location. French speaking personnel will only be available at test centers in Québec" (…/articles/5188825234591). French is also offered remotely (…/articles/5199211201439).
- **Closed book** [REGIME, re-verified]: "Documentation of any kind" is not permitted in the secure exam area (…/articles/5188773453215-What-can-I-take-with-me-on-exam-day). Paper and pens are not permitted; use the ProProctor scratch pad instead (…/articles/5199180904863).
- **Calculator** [REGIME, **now CONFIRMED** 2026-10-22]. Fitch Learning Support, "Can I use a physical calculator during the exam?" (updated 2026-10-17, https://fitchlearning-na.zendesk.com/hc/en-ca/articles/6075272480799-Can-I-use-a-physical-calculator-during-the-exam):
  - "personal calculators are not permitted under any circumstances."
  - "The online calculator provided by Prometric has been reviewed by our subject-matter experts and determined to be adequate for the purposes of the examination."
  - The article describes the calculator "within the ProProctor application" with "Basic and Scientific modes".
  
  So the exam uses an **on-screen basic/scientific calculator. It is not a financial calculator: there are no TVM keys.** Residual nuance [UNCONFIRMED]: the article shows the ProProctor (remote) calculator. It does not separately describe the test-centre interface, although the ban on personal calculators is unqualified.
  
  *This supersedes the "calculator policy UNCONFIRMED" entry in the Supervisor `exam-meta.json` (sources_verified 2026-06-04). The article post-dates that research.*

## 3. Official practice exam: format facts only (no content copied)

- **Source:** https://www.ciro.ca/media/13706/download?inline (local: `sources/Retail-Securities-Exam-Practice-Exam-EN.txt`, opened only for these counts).
- **Published:** the practice exam was released with Bulletin 25-0212 on 2025-07-21. The bulletin describes it as "an equivalent form of the exam with items drawn for an extensive question bank and that there will be 5 additional forms created for each examination type."
- **Length:** **120 questions**, numbered 1–120, on 57 pages: questions on pp. 1–36, answer key on pp. 37–57.
- **Options:** **4 per question (A–D)**. The text contains 480 option lines (120 × 4).
- **Answer key:** gives the question number, an item ID (format `RSEC_E_000xxx`) and the key letter only. **There are no rationales**, the same as the Supervisor practice exam.
- **Item sets / shared scenarios:** **none detected.** No grouped-question headers, no "questions X to Y" or "use the following" blocks, and no cross-references between questions. A few stems are long, with embedded tables or client profiles, but each is a single standalone item.
- **Calculations:** **yes.** About a fifth of the stems (24 of the 115 parsed automatically) carry numeric $ or % data, and several explicitly ask for a computed value. This is consistent with the calculator policy in §2.

## 4. Scoring [REGIME, re-verified 2026-10-22]

- **No pass mark is published.** CIRO FAQ, "Will CIRO publish the minimum passing grade?": "No. … there are different versions of each exam, each with its own passing grade. As such, publishing one passing grade will be misleading. Upon completion of an exam, individuals will receive a pass or fail mark." (https://www.ciro.ca/registered-individuals/proficiency/new-candidates)
- "Standard setting will be done according to psychometrically recognized methods. Standard setting is the methodology used to define a pass-score for each examination." (Bulletin 25-0212)
- Fitch, "What is the required grade to pass an exam?" (updated 2026-10-22): "Candidates receive a Pass or Fail result immediately after completing their test. Fitch Learning only administers the CIRO proficiency exams and does not have access to detailed scoring information." (https://fitchlearning-na.zendesk.com/hc/en-ca/articles/5365740605471)
- Exam Enrolment and Attempts Policy (v1, issued January 2026, CIRO DC-19220): results are given "immediately upon completion … available for review on the Candidate portal within 24 hours … overall result (pass/fail/absent). An absent result is considered a failed result." (https://fitchlearning-na.zendesk.com/hc/en-ca/articles/5227155152287-Exam-Enrolment-and-Attempts-Policy, attachment …/article_attachments/5275090353567)

## 6. Attempts, enrolment period, cooling-off [REGIME, re-verified 2026-10-22]

The syllabus itself states "Attempts allowed per exam 3" [RSE]. The Exam Enrolment and Attempts Policy (v1, Jan 2026) adds:
- **Enrolment period:** "the period from the date of Exam Enrolment up to a maximum of 12 months, during which the Candidate may complete up to three Exam Attempts for a specific exam."
- **Cooling-off period:** "the six-month period that occurs after three failed (or absent) Exam Attempts within six" months. During this period, enrolment, scheduling and further attempts are not permitted.
- **New enrolment period:** after the 12 months expire or 3 attempts are used, a new $475 Enrolment Fee starts a new enrolment period, "subject to the Cooling-off Period requirement."
- **Transfers:** "Enrolments are not transferable between individuals or different CIRO Exam types."
- **Rescheduling:** free if done "at least 36 hours prior to the time of the scheduled exam". It does not count as an attempt. Missing the exam without that notice results in "an absent (fail) result … and no refund".
- **Exceptional Circumstances:** exemptions are possible, with evidence.

## 8. Prerequisites / eligibility [REGIME, re-verified 2026-10-22]

- **No prerequisite courses.** "The new proficiency model does not require preparatory courses" (Exam Hub). Bulletin 25-0212 describes "an assessment centric model without pre-requisite courses".
- **No prerequisite exam to enrol.** Passing the CIRE first is **not** required to write the RSE. However, both exams are needed for RR (retail) approval under 2603(1)(i)(c).
- **Eligibility attestation** (Exam Enrolment and Attempts Policy, v1):
  - The candidate must be the age of majority.
  - The candidate must attest to enrolling either:
    - "(A) to demonstrate competency with respect to the work that I do or intend to do at a CIRO Dealer Member", and either work in such a role or plan to apply "within the next 12 months"; or
    - "(B) because I intend to pursue further CIRO exams for which this exam is a prerequisite."
  - "individuals who do not meet the conditions above, such as education providers, are not eligible to enrol for any CIRO Exam." Exceptions require written CIRO authorization in advance.
  - CIRO FAQ: "Is enrolment in the CIRO exam open to anyone? No."
- **Sponsorship:** not needed to *write* the exam. It is needed to be *approved*: "In order to apply to be a CIRO Approved Person, an individual must be sponsored by a CIRO Investment Dealer." (New Candidates FAQ)

## 9. Related exams

- **CIRE (Canadian Investment Regulatory Exam).** Required alongside the RSE for retail RRs (2603(1)(i)(c)/(d)).
  - Exam page: https://www.ciro.ca/registered-individuals/proficiency/exam-hub/canadian-investment-regulatory-exam-cire
  - **CIRE exemptions:** under Rule 2627(1), prior FINRA registration in a similar capacity within 3 years. Under Rule 2625(2)(i), 2 years' prior experience in the same Approved Person category within 3 years.
  - These exemptions cover the **CIRE only**. Neither one exempts the RSE.
- **Derivatives Exam.** An add-on for retail RRs dealing in derivatives (2603(1)(i)(d)): https://www.ciro.ca/registered-individuals/proficiency/exam-hub/derivatives-exam
- **Institutional Securities Exam.** The institutional counterpart (2603(1)(i)(e)/(f)).
- **Supervisor Exam.** Supervisors of retail RRs must also hold the RSE (2603(1)(i)(i)(I)).
- **Competency profile (RSE basis):**
  - Bulletin 25-0212 Appendix 1, "Competency Profile – Retail Registered Representatives and Investment Representatives": https://www.ciro.ca/media/13641/download?inline

## 10. Validity and post-approval [RSE rule refs, from IDPC text in corpus]

**Rule 2628(1), exam validity.** An exam is deemed completed if any one of the following holds:
- (i) it was passed "within three years prior to the date of application for approval";
- (ii) the person was previously approved in the same category, or in another category requiring the same exam, within 3 years;
- (iii) the person "gained one year of relevant securities industry experience, acceptable to the Corporation, within three years prior to the date of application for approval."

Rule 2628(2) adds that suspended periods, or periods not conducting approved activities, do not count as approved.

**Post-approval requirements:**
- **Rule 2604(1)(i), dealer training:** the Dealer Member must train RRs and IRs "within 90 days after approval considering the type of client and product", and notify CIRO within 90 days.
- **Rule 2604(2)(i), Conduct Training:** must be completed "within 30 days after approval". It is free: Bulletin 25-0205 says it "will not be subject to any fees" (from the Supervisor research).
- **Rule 2704(1), continuing education:** a Registered Representative serving retail clients has a **Compliance course requirement: Yes** and a **Professional development course requirement: Yes**. Supervisors need compliance only.

## Unconfirmed / caveats

1. **Item sets.** No official statement explicitly says the RSE has none. The syllabus says only "Multiple choice", and the practice exam contains only standalone items. The working assumption is 120 standalone items.
2. **Calculator at test centres.** The official article describes the ProProctor on-screen calculator (Basic/Scientific). The test-centre interface is not separately described, though the ban on personal calculators is unconditional. The calculator is **not** a financial (TVM-key) calculator, so TVM questions must be solvable with a scientific calculator.
3. **Pass mark.** It is deliberately unpublished, and each form has its own standard. Never display an "official pass threshold."
4. **Guidance-note numbering** (e.g. `GN-2600-25-001`). Per the project's CLAUDE.md this numbering remains officially unconfirmed, so the guidance is cited by URL/title only.
5. **Original Guide for Studying PDF not saved to `downloads/`.** ciro.ca blocks non-browser downloads (HTTP 403), so the text was extracted in-browser with pdf.js from https://www.ciro.ca/media/13546/download?inline (560,996 bytes). Save the PDF manually if the original is needed.
6. Facts marked [REGIME] without "re-verified" were carried over from the Supervisor research of 2026-06-04: Prometric's CIRO page, Bulletin 25-0205's free Conduct Training statement, and the refund timing details.
