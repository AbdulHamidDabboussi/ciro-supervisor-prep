**Two versions are pointed to:**

- **OLD** = shared corpus `research/sources/rules/idpc-rules.txt` (IDPC consolidation **January 8, 2026**) and `research/sources/rules/umir.txt` (annotated UMIR to **January 13, 2026**). Paths relative to repo root.
- **NEW** = `exams/retail-securities/research/sources/ciro/idpc-rules-2026-08-11.txt` (IDPC consolidation **August 11, 2026**, current on ciro.ca as of 2026-10-22) and `.../ciro/umir-2026-08-11.txt` (annotated UMIR to **August 11, 2026**, current). Same extraction method; **drafters should quote from NEW** (it is the version in force).

Material differences found between OLD and NEW (section-by-section whitespace-insensitive comparison of the IDPC; UMIR regulatory-history notes):
- IDPC **4780–4782 new** (Part C of Rule 4700 – reasonable expectation to settle a short sale for non-Participant Dealer Members), effective Aug 11, 2026; UMIR **3.3 amended** effective Aug 11, 2026 (CIRO notice "Provisions Respecting Reasonable Expectations to Settle Short Sales", April 2, 2026).
- IDPC Rule 4600 Part B.2 new (4620–4630, borrowing retail clients' fully paid / excess margin securities); 5840/5850 removed (moved to 4600).
- IDPC 3802: defined term "cost" renamed "position cost" (account statements / performance reporting); 3811 and many margin tables show only extraction-layout differences. Rules 1400, 3100–3700 (other than above), 7100 are textually unchanged.
- UMIR: many guidance cross-references updated (UMIR Guidance Update Project, Feb–Mar 2026); no other substantive rule changes identified in the retail-relevant Parts 2, 4, 5, 10.16.

Margin-account and **cash-account** client rules partly live outside the Rules text, in **IDPC Form 1, Schedule 4 Notes** (excerpt: `ciro/idpc-form-1-schedule-4-cash-and-margin-accounts.txt`, Form 1 dated 2026-04-27).
